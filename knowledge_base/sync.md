# Sync and devices

## How sync works
Notes sync through Lumen Cloud whenever a device is online. Changes usually appear on other devices within 10 seconds. Pro users can keep working offline; changes upload the next time the device connects.

## Sync is stuck
If a note shows "Waiting to sync" for more than a few minutes: check the device is online, then pull down on the notes list (mobile) or press Ctrl+R / Cmd+R (desktop) to force a sync. If it is still stuck, sign out and back in. Signing out never deletes notes that already synced.

## Conflicts
If the same note is edited on two offline devices, Lumen keeps both versions and marks the newer one "Conflict copy". Open both and keep the one you want; nothing is lost.

## Device limit
Free accounts can sign in on 2 devices at once. Signing in on a third device asks you to sign out of one of the others. Pro has no device limit.
