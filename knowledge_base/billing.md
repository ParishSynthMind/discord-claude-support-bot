# Billing

## Plans and prices
Lumen Notes has two plans. Free covers 3 notebooks and 2 devices. Pro costs 6 US dollars a month or 60 US dollars a year and gives unlimited notebooks, unlimited devices, version history and offline mode. Prices shown in the app include any sales tax for your country.

## Refunds
Annual Pro plans can be refunded in full within 14 days of the payment. Monthly plans are not refunded, but you can cancel at any time and keep Pro until the end of the month you paid for. Refunds go back to the card or PayPal account that paid, and take 5 to 10 working days to arrive. Refunds are handled by the support team, not in the app.

## Cancelling
Cancel from Settings > Account > Subscription > Cancel plan. If you subscribed through the App Store or Google Play, cancel there instead, because Lumen can't change those subscriptions. After cancelling, your notes stay; notebooks above the Free limit become read-only.

## Student discount
Students get 50 percent off the annual Pro plan with a valid school email address. Apply from Settings > Account > Student discount. The discount lasts one year and can be renewed.
