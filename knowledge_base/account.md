# Account

## Resetting your password
Use "Forgot password" on the sign-in screen. The reset link is sent to your account email and works for 30 minutes. If the email doesn't arrive, check spam and wait 5 minutes before asking for another one.

## Two-factor authentication
Turn on two-factor authentication in Settings > Security with any authenticator app. Save the 10 backup codes shown when you turn it on. If you lose both your phone and the backup codes, only the support team can recover the account, after checking your identity.

## Exporting your notes
Settings > Data > Export creates a zip of all notes as Markdown files with their attachments. Exports are free on every plan.

## Deleting your account
Settings > Account > Delete account removes your account and all notes after a 7-day grace period. Signing back in during those 7 days cancels the deletion.
